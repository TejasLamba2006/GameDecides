# GameDecides

**Random twists that keep your gameplay exciting!** ⚡🎲

Ever wanted to spice up your Minecraft sessions? GameDecides throws random curveballs at your players every few minutes - keeping everyone on their toes!

---

## 🎮 What Happens?

Every 5 minutes (configurable), a new "twist" activates:

- 🌙 **Ghost Mode** - Everyone goes invisible!
- 💨 **Low Gravity** - Jump to the moon!
- 🔥 **Fire Touch** - Everything you hit catches fire!
- 🎰 **Inventory Swap** - Your items switch with someone else!
- 💀 **One Tap** - All hits are instant kills!
- 🥔 **Hot Potato** - Pass it or explode!

*...and 40+ more twists!*

## 🔧 Fully Customizable

- ✅ Enable/disable any twist
- ⏱️ Adjust timing between twists
- 🌍 Blacklist specific worlds
- 🎵 Custom sounds and titles
- 📝 Customize all messages

---

## 📋 Quick Start

1. Drop in plugins folder
2. Restart server
3. Run `/gamedecides start`
4. Watch the chaos unfold! 🎉
